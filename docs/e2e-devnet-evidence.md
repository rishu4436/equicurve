## Known limitations (documented, not changed)
- **ExactIn near curve completion:** a buy whose input exceeds the remaining curve fails with DBC `6033 Insufficient Liquidity` instead of partially filling. The suite shrinks the amount and retries. A UI "buy remaining" helper would help, but that is a feature and out of scope.
- **Preset market caps are quote-denominated** but labelled "Usd" in preset code. The short-raise preset with SOL quote needs 772.5 SOL to complete, so it is realistic only with a 6-dec quote.
- **DBC-migrated DAMM v2 pools are always tokenA = base.** The app handles both orders (`pickMints`), and the reverse order was exercised with a custom pool.
- Transfer-hook mode is untested on-chain (no hook program available).
