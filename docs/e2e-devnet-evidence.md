## Read this first: where these transactions ran

**These transactions are NOT on public devnet, and the links below will not resolve on the devnet explorer.**
The public devnet faucet (`requestAirdrop` on api.devnet.solana.com) returned `429 airdrop limit reached` for every fresh keypair from this machine's IP for the whole session. A retry loop asked for 1 SOL per keypair every 15 minutes and got nothing. Every other free faucet needs a login or a captcha, and paid services were out of scope. ## Known limitations
- ~~ExactIn near curve completion fails with DBC 6033~~ **Fixed in PASS 3:** `planBuy` (`src/lib/dbc/fill.ts`) switches to `SwapMode.PartialFill` when the input would overshoot the curve, and 6033 now maps to an actionable message. Proven by rows 8 and 11.
- ~~Preset market caps labelled "Usd"; SOL short preset needed 772.5 SOL~~ **Fixed in PASS 3:** presets carry separate SOL and USDC market caps; the SOL short threshold is 3.09 SOL (row 11).
- **DBC-migrated DAMM v2 pools are always tokenA = base.** The app handles both orders (`pickMints`), and the reverse order was exercised with a custom pool.
- Transfer-hook mode is untested on-chain (no hook program available).
